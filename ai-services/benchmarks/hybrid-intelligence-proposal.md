# Proposed hybrid matching experiment

Keep the deterministic requirement evaluator and coverage tiers authoritative. Correct the demonstrated evidence errors and establish a section-aware lexical control first. In a planner-approved shadow run, compare that control with current MiniLM relevance **within the same verified requirement tier**. Run it only for fresh evaluations in a comparison cohort with equivalent mandatory evidence. Preserve deterministic order whenever a neural result is unavailable, truncated, falls back, or the relevance comparison is inconclusive. A similarity score cannot change a requirement status or supply evidence of seniority, recency, or proficiency.

## What the benchmarks measure

The existing 75-pair fixture measures heuristic domain alignment. Its 20 resume IDs are isolated by split, while all eight job IDs recur across splits. The 50-case extraction fixture measures section presence against structured source columns. The 30-pair semantic fixture has 30 candidate IDs and 12 job IDs with no cross-split ID overlap. Its labels describe ordinal topical alignment on candidate–job pairs. The A4 challenge groups profiles by requisition and evaluates evidence, topical comparisons, and explicit job-requirement contrasts separately.

In a current offline rerun of the 30 curated pairs, the baseline displayed 100 for 7 of 30 pairs, all among the 12 strong pairs. Its raw cosine ranged from 0.0000 to 0.7532. The baseline's 1.8 multiplier caps its index at 100 once cosine reaches approximately 0.556. MiniLM reached 100 on 0 of 30 pairs, with raw cosine from 0.0000 to 0.8223. All 30 pairs per engine completed successfully. The dense model can still assign similar scores to candidates with different qualifications. Report raw similarity for analysis and use the presentation index for display.

On the 22-pair challenge, MiniLM ordered 11 of 11 topical contrasts correctly and raw baseline cosine ordered 10 of 11 with one tie. Both raw scores got 7 of 10 explicit qualification contrasts right. In an offline simulation that sorted profiles with all mandatory requirements supported first, then by raw relevance, MiniLM got 9 of 10 and baseline got 8 of 10. Both reversed the Go comparison because `Golang` was missed and `go-live` was promoted to supported. The sample exposes a dependency on evidence quality and gives one additional correctly ordered contrast for gated MiniLM over gated baseline. ## Options

| Option | Change and expected benefit | Benchmark rationale | Cost, fallback, and risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Within-tier relevance with mandatory evidence gating | Evaluate the current model asynchronously in shadow mode. Compare the existing deterministic order with an evidence-tier-first order using raw neural relevance for eligible comparisons. This can recover topical matches with lower lexical overlap. | The challenge directly measures topical order and contrasts it with explicit requirements. Test the incremental neural benefit against the same gate with raw deterministic relevance. | One candidate encode per evaluated comparison and reusable job vectors. Uses the current model and dependencies. Medium integration complexity for gating, feature telemetry, versioning, and comparison cohorts. Keep the deterministic result on timeout, error, insufficient evidence, or unavailable model. High lexical or dense overlap with copied requisitions remains a relevance false-positive risk even within the same tier. |
| Selective embedding-based corroboration | Retrieve a small number of relevant candidate passages for recruiter review and show verbatim context. Keep current requirement decisions independent of retrieved passages. | The challenge includes alternative terminology, ambiguous words, and contextual claims. Its pair metrics do not establish passage retrieval precision. Add independently labeled passages before use. | Can reuse normalized MiniLM embeddings within the request. Per-requirement query encoding and excerpt selection add work beyond the measured whole-profile comparison. Medium integration complexity. On failure, omit passages and retain deterministic evidence. Quotes and third-party statements can rank highly without contextual validation. |
| Transferable skill retrieval | Retrieve plausible nonidentical skills from a vetted taxonomy and require review of the original source span. Keep transfers distinct from exact evidence. | The challenge includes exact requirements, alternative names for the same technology, and cross-domain transfer. It provides no measured retrieval gain. Prioritize explicit alias normalization for unambiguous equivalents. | Existing MiniLM can embed taxonomy entries offline. Online cost depends on query count and retrieval set size. Medium integration complexity for taxonomy governance and context validation. Fall back to curated mappings and deterministic spans. Semantic neighbors may be unsafe substitutes for mandatory tools or credentials. |
| Cross-encoder reranking, replacement, or fine-tuning | Consider a bounded reranker only if an established in-tier failure persists after fixing evidence and input coverage. A replacement or fine-tune would require a stronger reference set and an independent comparison. | A4 measured the current pretrained MiniLM only. There is no measured gain or latency for these alternatives, and a more expensive model cannot repair missing deterministic evidence by itself. | Pairwise inference adds work per candidate/job comparison and reduces cache reuse. New models add weights, dependency and provenance review, and operational maintenance. Training adds labeling and evaluation costs. Keep deterministic fallback. Negation and copied descriptions remain false-positive risks until tested. |

## Shadow-run acceptance checks

1. Require the backend to preserve source spans, evidence provenance, and required versus preferred categories through persistence and both result projections. The backend diagnostic payload currently sends `all_candidate_skills`, a union of declared and extracted skills, to the AI-service `skills` field. Use the authoritative backend evidence for gating, and preserve declarations and extracted mentions separately in any future corroboration request. [Backend regression checklist](backend-evidence-regression.md)
2. Compare deterministic raw relevance and MiniLM raw relevance with the same evidence gate and text access. Giving the lexical scorer the section text offline yielded 11 of 11 topical contrasts and 9 of 10 qualification contrasts with the gate, tying MiniLM on both. Measure same-requisition order within each tier and count cross-tier inversions on new examples. Include overlap with unresolved mandatory evidence, copied postings, irrelevant usage, and strong transferable profiles.
3. Before any production trial, use separately reviewed relevance and requirement labels. Group candidates and requisitions to prevent either identity from crossing calibration and evaluation partitions. Record rank changes, false-positive examples, latency percentiles, coverage, and fallback frequency. Keep the current service response, ranking weights, model, and ordering for the shadow run.
